# extras.md — additional information (long-form, PLAN §5.2)

Anything that doesn't fit structured YAML but should be available to the
tailoring/cover-letter layers and to you during applications:

- Compensation philosophy and history context (the structured policy lives in
  resume.yaml `extras.salary` — the *why* lives here).
- Relocation constraints, family/logistics context you choose to share.
- What you want from your next role — team shape, ownership, growth path.
- References policy (who, when, with permission).
- Anything you want every cover letter to *not* say (tone constraints).

This file is read by the quality tier when composing packets. Keep it honest;
the claim-check gate treats resume.yaml as the authority for facts — this file
is context, not claims.
