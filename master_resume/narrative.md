# narrative.md — STAR stories keyed to bullet IDs (PLAN §5.2)

Raw material for cover letters and interview prep. Each story references
master_resume bullets by ID so generated text can be grounded. Never pasted
wholesale into anything. Aim for 6–10 stories across your strongest themes.

Template:

    ## STORY-S1 (refs: EXP1-B2, EXP1-B4)

    **Situation** — what was going on, why it mattered.
    **Task** — what you were responsible for.
    **Action** — what you actually did (technical detail welcome).
    **Result** — measured outcome. Must match the `metrics:` in the referenced
    bullets — the claim-check gate verifies this.
